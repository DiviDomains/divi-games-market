      How the season market works
      • Each season has its own two tokens, SnGOLD and SnDIVI. Joining gives you the season's starting balance of both; you can join each season once, with an address that holds none of its tokens.
      • A buy offers SnGOLD for SnDIVI at your price or better; a sell offers SnDIVI. Price is in whole SnGOLD per whole SnDIVI, with up to 8 decimals.
      • An order that meets the other side trades at once at the resting order's price; whatever is left rests on the book until it fills or you cancel it. A buy locks its SnGOLD while it rests; cancelling returns it.
      • Orders that would trade with your own resting order are refused. At most 100 open orders per address.
      • Trading is open from the season's start block to its end block. The leaderboard values what you hold at the last trade price.
      • When the season ends, an order still resting can no longer fill. It stays on the book with what it holds, and still counts in your value, until you cancel it; a cancel works after the end and returns it to you.
      • You can play here by hand, or let a program trade for you: download the example Python bot and change its strategy.
